Poo Leaking Procedure

Evacuate the pool for Level 3 only
  1. Identify the critical level (see table).
  2. Level 1 or 2: clear the debris directly and isolate from pool.
  3. Level 3 only: evacuate the pool.
  4. Clear the debris with vacuum and net ASAP.
  5. Dose 100g CL after clearing all debris.
  6. Cancel class for 30 mins after dosing for pool buffer, then start class after that.
